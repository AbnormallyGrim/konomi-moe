Browser extension: toolbar popup and AniList badge

The extension-only surfaces. Same public AniList titles as the app mockup, never a user's list.

The logo isn't decided yet. The grey square is a placeholder everywhere on this page.

1 · Toolbar popup

A quick glance, not the app: your top picks, a verdict for the AniList title you're looking at, and a button to open the full app in its own tab. Click the toolbar icon to close or open it.

State
anilist.co/anime/20954/A-Silent-Voice

The pink count on the icon means new picks since you last looked. It's off by default (Settings), like the page badge, so the extension stays quiet until asked.

2 · Badge on AniList title pages (opt-in)

A small verdict under AniList's "Add to List" button, in words rather than a number (there's no fit score anywhere). Click it for the reasons and actions. Off until you switch it on, and that's the moment the browser asks for access to anilist.co.

Every badge state

Verdicts come from where the title would rank among your candidates: top tenth "Great match", top third "Good match", the rest "Mixed", and titles the model expects you to dislike "Not your usual". Titles already on your list get no verdict, just a line saying so. Too few scored titles to judge shows "Not enough to go on".
